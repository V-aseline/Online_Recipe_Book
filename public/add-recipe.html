Before You Submit

Make sure your recipe is clear and easy for others to follow.

  • Use a clear and descriptive recipe name.
  • Choose the correct recipe category.
  • Include accurate ingredient quantities.
  • Write easy-to-follow cooking steps.
  • Add a photo of your finished dish.
